Important Instructions for Creators

Please read the following Payment & Return Policy carefully.

  1. Payment Hold: When a customer purchases your craft, the payment will not be transferred to your account immediately.
  2. Admin Payment Management: The customer's payment will initially be held by Kalasetu Admin.
  3. 2-Day Return Period: Customers will have 2 days to request a return after receiving their order.
  4. Payment Release: If the customer does not request a return within the 2-day period, the eligible payment will be released to your account.
  5. Return Requests: If a customer raises a return request within the return period, the payment will remain on hold until the request is resolved according to Kalasetu's return policy.
  6. Creator Agreement: Please read and understand all the above instructions before submitting your creator verification.

Your details will be reviewed by the Kalasetu admin before creator access is approved.
